<h1>Microsoft Sales Breakdown</h1>
<p><b>Features</b></p>
<p>The first card shows us the total profit earned by Microsoft. The second card shows the average revenue, and the last shows the company's total sales.</p>
<p>You can filter by product, customer segmentation and year.</p>
<p>In the line chart, sales in the first two quarters are declining, but in the third quarter they increase dratistically and continue to rise.</p>
<p>On the weekly profit Monday has the highest profit.</p>
<p>The tree map shows the highest sales in the region. Europe, Africa, Asia and the Middle East have almost the same amount of sales, while North America has the lowest sales</p>
<p>In the donut chart, we can see that Hardware has the highest average revenue compared to other categories.</p>
